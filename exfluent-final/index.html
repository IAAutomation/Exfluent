How much money will I actually save?

The average Exfluent user saves $89/month per active AI seat by identifying redundant subscriptions and optimizing token usage through our proprietary intelligence routing layer. High-volume agencies typically see savings exceeding 30% of their total AI overhead within the first billing cycle.

add

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. Exfluent uses bank-level AES-256 encryption for all data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. We are SOC2 Type II compliant and never train our models on your sensitive business logic or proprietary audit logs. Your data remains yours, localized to your workspace.

add

Which AI tools does Exfluent support?

Exfluent supports all major AI tools including ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, Jasper, and over 45+ enterprise-grade LLM providers. Our API integrations allow for real-time monitoring of seat utilization and cost-per-prompt metrics across your entire tech stack.

add

How long does setup take?

Most users complete setup in under 60 seconds. Simply connect your primary billing accounts or invite your team members, and our AI will immediately begin auditing your historical usage patterns to generate your first Efficiency Score and ROI report.

add

Can I cancel my Exfluent subscription anytime?

Your Pro or Team subscription continues until the end of your billing period. You can downgrade to the Free tier at any time through your account settings. We believe in earning your business through performance, not lock-in contracts.

add

Does Exfluent work for teams and agencies?

Yes. Our Team plan includes admin dashboards specifically designed for agencies to manage client-specific AI spend. You can set budget caps per project, track individual contributor efficiency, and export white-labeled ROI reports for your stakeholders.
