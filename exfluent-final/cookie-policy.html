1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or device when you load websites in a browser. They are widely utilized to make web platforms function, track authentication sessions, remember preferences, and provide analytical usage reports to site owners.

2. How Exfluent Uses Cookies

Exfluent uses cookies for the following primary objectives:

  • Essential Cookies: Required to keep you authenticated inside your subscription dashboard panels.
  • Performance Cookies: Collect information about how you interact with our tracking features to optimize loading speed.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember custom settings, such as currency preference, dark mode choice, or dashboard layout selections.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may include service tools (e.g., analytical processors, secure checkouts) that place their own tracking items in your browser. These cookies enable third parties to gather data assets in accordance with their own specific privacy parameters.

4. Managing Your Cookie Settings

Most browsers let you reject or remove cookies via built-in setting dialogs. Note that blocking essential cookies will prevent the Exfluent dashboard session tracking from functioning correctly, requiring manual login on each page refresh.
