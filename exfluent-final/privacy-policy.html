Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Terms of Service Privacy Policy Cookie Policy Security

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when setting up an Exfluent profile or linking active AI workspace statistics. This includes email addresses, payment profile descriptions, and SaaS subscription invoice data uploaded to evaluate overlap waste.

2. How We Use Information

Exfluent uses the collected data to calculate subscription optimization opportunities, display interactive usage reports, notify users about renewal dates, aggregate usage trends across tool sets, and process transaction histories accurately.

3. Subscription and Billing Data

We do not store complete bank account details or credit card credentials. All financial processing is safely delegated to our secure payment gateway providers. Subscription billing trends and metrics are visual-only datasets designed for tracking ROI scores.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Integrations

We never sell your analytical workspace statistics or user profiles to data brokers. We share details only to process operations (such as email systems, hosting, payment processors) or when you explicitly configure integrations with third-party tools (like GitHub Copilot or Claude).

5. Data Retention

We store your account statistics for as long as your profile remains active. Upon request, we will permanently delete all data assets associated with your profile, except where compliance logs or transaction details are legally required to be preserved.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have full access to view, update, correct, or request deletion of personal information stored within our database panels. You can manage newsletter settings or disable specific SaaS integration hooks at any time directly through the dashboard interface.
